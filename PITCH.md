# 3-minute pitch script

**0:00 Hook.** "Every AI today *talks*. Pragati AI *finishes*. Ask. Align. Act."

**0:10 Open the app.** Enter the demo workspace and let the warp intro play. "One interface, five ways to move forward: Personal, Work, Study, Life, Agent."

**0:30 The loop.** Click **▶ Autopilot demo** (or say/type it): *"Plan my study, finish my notes, remind me at 7."*
Show: 3 steps detected, **nothing has run yet**, each step has a *why*, click Approve, steps complete live, **Undo** one.
"No mystery automation. Every step has a state, a reason and a way to stop."

**1:40 Language.** Type `kal physics aur maths padhna hai, notes bana do aur shaam 7 baje yaad dilao`. "Hinglish, understood."

**2:00 Honesty.** Type `Clear completed tasks and call mom`. Clear-tasks needs an explicit confirm; "call mom" shows **Not available yet**. "We don't pretend. Calls need telephony APIs and consent; it's on the roadmap."

**2:25 Trust.** Trust Center, **Verify chain**. "Every action is in a tamper-evident ledger. Export or erase everything in one click."

**2:50 Close.** "A real prototype that completes one meaningful task reliably, which is exactly the milestone in our roadmap."

## Likely judge questions
- **Is the AI real?** Planning runs on a deterministic offline engine; with an API key it switches to Claude/OpenAI and the UI shows which is active.
- **Why not just ChatGPT?** It acts, not just answers: plan, preview, approve, undo, with an audit trail.
- **Privacy?** Minimum data, no biometrics stored, transcripts opt-out, full export/erase.
- **What's next?** Phase 04 Test: task success, errors and time saved are already counted live on the console.
